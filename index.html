1 Core Privacy Commitments

Mak Device Doctor is designed as a local-first smartphone hardware diagnostics application. We do not operate application-owned backend servers to collect, store, or analyze your diagnostic results.

No User Accounts No registration, login, email sign-up, or user profiles are required.
No App Backend Servers Diagnostic test results are stored locally on your device, not uploaded to our servers.
No Location / GPS Access The app does not request GPS or location permissions.
No Personal Data Access No access to contacts, SMS messages, call logs, or personal files.

2 Hardware & Device Information Accessed

To perform interactive hardware diagnostics and display system telemetry, Mak Device Doctor accesses the following device information locally on your phone:

  • Device Identity & OS Info: Device manufacturer, model name, Android version, API level, and hardware platform identifier.
  • Battery Information: Battery percentage, charging status, power source, health status, temperature, voltage, and live charging current.
  • Storage Capacity: Internal and removable storage totals, used/free space, and a temporary read/write probe file inside the app's private cache directory that is deleted immediately after verification.
  • Hardware Sensor Readings: Live readings from available sensors (such as accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, proximity, light, barometer, gravity, linear acceleration, and rotation vector) while the Sensors diagnostic screen is active.
  • Camera Preview & Autofocus: Camera hardware availability, live camera preview, autofocus verification, and flash/torch testing. The camera is used strictly for live preview and hardware testing — not for capturing or saving photos or videos.
  • Temporary Microphone Input: Real-time audio input levels (dBFS) and temporary in-memory PCM audio for immediate loopback verification. Microphone audio is processed temporarily in memory and is never uploaded or saved to external storage.

3 Android Permissions

Mak Device Doctor requests only the permissions required for hardware testing and Google advertising/consent services:

Permission Type Purpose
CAMERA Runtime (Optional) Used only when you run the Camera Diagnostic screen for live preview, autofocus, and flash/torch testing.
RECORD_AUDIO Runtime (Optional) Used only when you run the Microphone Diagnostic screen to measure live audio input levels in memory.
INTERNET Normal Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK Next-Gen and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent configurations and load ads.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK Next-Gen and UMP to check network connectivity before making ad or consent requests.

4 Local Storage & PDF Report Generation

Your diagnostic session results and app preferences (selected language and theme mode) are stored locally in private Android SharedPreferences on your device.

When you generate a Full Device Report PDF, the document is created locally on your device and saved exclusively in the application's private cache directory (cacheDir/reports/). PDF files are only opened or shared with external apps when you explicitly choose Open PDF or Share PDF via Android's secure FileProvider mechanism.

Temporary PDF reports and cache files are excluded from Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfers.

5 Third-Party Advertising Services

Mak Device Doctor integrates official Google advertising and consent libraries:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK Next-Gen (version 1.6.0)
  • Google User Messaging Platform — UMP (version 4.0.0)

Supported Ad Formats

  • Banner Ads: Displayed on the Dashboard screen only.
  • Interstitial Ads: Displayed only at natural transition points after returning from diagnostic workflows, subject to frequency limits.
  • Rewarded Ads: Optionally watched by the user to unlock PDF Report export.

When ad requests are permitted by your consent settings, Google advertising services may automatically process device, advertising identifier (such as Advertising ID or App Set ID), network/IP address, coarse location inferred from IP, and ad interaction information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6 Consent Management & Privacy Options

Mak Device Doctor uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0 to check applicable privacy requirements on app launch and present consent forms where required by regional regulations (such as GDPR in the EEA/UK or applicable US state privacy laws).

The Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialized and permitted to request ads only after UMP consent checks confirm that ad requests are allowed under your current consent state.

Where required by applicable regulations, a Privacy Options entry is automatically displayed inside the application's Settings screen so you can review or update your advertising consent preferences at any time.

7 Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Diagnostic Results & Preferences: Retained locally on your device until overwritten by new tests, cleared via Android Settings > Apps > Mak Device Doctor > Storage > Clear storage, or removed when you uninstall the application.
  • PDF Reports in App Cache: Retained temporarily in the app's private cache directory and automatically subject to Android system cache cleanup, or removable anytime via Android Clear cache or uninstalling the app.
  • Temporary Storage & Audio Data: Storage read/write probe files are deleted immediately after each check; microphone diagnostic audio is held in memory only during the test.
  • Google Advertising & Consent Data: Data processed by Google AdMob and UMP is handled and retained separately by Google in accordance with Google's privacy policies and your consent choices.

8 Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Mak Device Doctor is a general-utility smartphone hardware diagnostics tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates, SDK upgrades, or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
